## 1. Architecture Decision

- **Context:** Constitution §3 — "a new concern is a new file, not an edit to the roles" — is the spine, and the lens mechanism (`lenses/README.md`) is the contract to conform to. No runtime, no build, no test suite (§4); the bar is a documented dogfood, **negative case first** (§1). Two mechanics had to be verified against the files rather than taken from the spec's paraphrase, and both were:
  - **Characteristic- vs type-triggered, mechanically.** `lenses/README.md:45-49` and `lenses/security.md:1-5` are unambiguous: a type-triggered lens declares `applies-to: [<type>]` (`ux`, `seo`), a characteristic-triggered one declares `triggers: [<characteristic>]` (`security`, `i18n`, `data`) — **exactly one of the two keys**, never both. The difference is not just the key name: activation is sourced from a different row of the constitution's §2 profile (*Characteristics*, not *Project type(s)*) and requires a **new row in `lenses/README.md`'s Characteristics detection-signals table** (`:82-91`), which a type-triggered lens would not need. `security.md` is the shape to mirror: frontmatter → an activation paragraph explaining *why* the concern is a property and not a shape → a "does not repeat the baseline, deepens it" paragraph → the "flags, never invents scope" line → a *Who owns what* table (Area | Owner phase | Agent) → a checklist whose every `###` section carries its owner phase in italics → *Phase notes*, one bullet per phase in `phases`.
  - **C6 verified, holds.** `skills/increment/SKILL.md:27-29` reads, verbatim: *"From `spec.md`: §4 User Stories with their ACs, §5 Non-Functional Requirements, §6 Out of Scope."* No category filter, no conditional, no lens resolution step anywhere in the file. An accessibility `NFR-n` grounded at Specify is consumed by `/increment` exactly like a security or performance one. **Zero edits to `skills/increment/SKILL.md`.** The caveat worth writing down: `/increment` never reads lens files *at all*, so the Act row exists only transitively through the NFR that Specify grounded — if AC-2.1 lands as the one-line N/A, the Act row silently has nothing to attach to. That shapes the lens text (R5) rather than the file list.
  - **What C6's neighbours do *not* do (new finding, §2).** The spec asked the add-a-file question only of `/increment`. Asked of the other four phases: `story-time:41` passes lenses generically (Specify ✅); `peer-review:50-51` keeps the generic `<name>.md` placeholder with a parenthetical membership list (Review ✅ — the ruling `situational-lenses` Entry 3 already made, though the parenthetical will not name `accessibility`); but `look-and-feel/SKILL.md:34-35` ("*the design-relevant ones are `ux`, `seo` (content structure) and `i18n` (text expansion, RTL) — pass those paths*") and `demo-day/SKILL.md:84` ("*`ux.md`, `seo.md`, `security.md`, `i18n.md`*") are **closed enumerations**: the named files *are* the instruction. Both lists are accurate for today's eight lenses and stop being accurate the moment a ninth declares `design` or `qa`. This lens declares both. `demo-day:84` is the finding `situational-lenses` recorded as `refuted-with-finding` and routed to "a later increment"; `look-and-feel:34-35` is **new** — Entry 3's grep (`CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT.*lenses`) could not see it, because the closed list sits in the *selection* step, one step above the generic `<name>.md` hand-off in `:42`. Agents are clean: `designer.md:62-74`, `reviewer.md:62-79`, `qa-tester.md:174-193` all open with the generic "for each active lens the caller passed, read it and apply its slice" frame and list per-lens summaries only as a digest — incomplete digests are a Nit, not a dispatch failure, and no agent file is edited.
- **Decision:** One new lens file mirroring `security.md` structurally, its two registry rows in `lenses/README.md`, and the two pointer edits — plus, under constitution §4, one row and one clause in root `README.md` (Q1). The two dispatch defects are **recorded, not repaired**: `.spark/accessibility-lens/evidence.md` carries the refutation with `file:line` and routes it to a follow-up increment, and the lens ships knowing its Design and QA rows are not yet dispatched. The negative case (suppression on this repo's own `library` profile, no `must-be-accessible`) runs at T2, before any positive claim, and again at T9 after the registry rows land.

## 4. Test Strategy

No automated suite exists or is possible for prompt material (constitution §4); the bar is `claude plugin validate` passing plus a documented dogfood, **negative case first** (§1). Three layers:

- **Layer 1 — artifact conformance (T1, T3, T4, T5, T6).** Every DoD is a read-and-count against a quoted rule: frontmatter keys present and `applies-to` absent; owner-phase↔`phases` membership checked in both directions (5/5); the no-duplication claim checked as a grep for `4.5:1`/`44px` outside the single citation; the pointer edits checked as a 1-line `git diff --stat` per file. These are re-runnable by the Reviewer without a browser.
- **Layer 2 — the negative case, performed (T2, re-run at T9).** Constitution §1's "in a repo where the capability is absent, nothing may change", performed on aSPARK Core itself: `library`, characteristics "none of the six". The entry must state its counting domain, because a zero whose domain is unstated is indistinguishable from nothing having been looked at (R1) — the exact failure `situational-lenses` R-C built its positive control against. There is no positive control available here (see below), so the domain statement is the only thing standing between this row and vacuity, and `/peer-review` should attack it on that basis.
- **Layer 3 — the dispatch audit (T7, T9).** `git diff --name-only` and the quoted `file:line` of each closed enumeration. This layer is what makes NFR-6 falsifiable rather than asserted, and it is the layer that returns a refutation rather than a pass.

**Deliberately left `not-verified-live`, with the venue named in the row, not implied:** the whole positive half — a project declaring `must-be-accessible` where `/story-time` grounds an accessibility `NFR-n`, `/peer-review` traces it to a `file:line`, and `/demo-day` verifies it keyboard-only with a measured contrast ratio. aSPARK Core has no browser-observable surface (constitution §8) and declares no UI-bearing characteristic, so **no venue exists on this machine** (spec C4, NFR-Provability, R2). A synthetic fixture is rejected on the record: it would prove the mechanism can fire and then be filed as the claim that it fired — precisely `situational-lenses`'s rejected shortcut (its plan §1) and the R1 failure this repo keeps naming. `/demo-day` for this feature runs under constitution §8's substitute method: its performed steps are re-executing Layer 1's greps and re-running Layer 2's negative case in a fresh session; every positive-half AC lands `not-verified-live` in `qa.md` with the missing venue named, pending a field report.
