## 1. Architecture Decision

- **Context:** **Eight** sites across seven files encode the lens/characteristic vocabulary as closed lists — the spec's six, plus two more of the identical shape found at plan time (`skills/spark/SKILL.md:66`, `templates/spec.md:69`, both an 8-name lens list omitting `accessibility`). Two distinct questions are being answered badly: *which characteristics/lenses exist* (`/charter` and its feeder sites, US-1) and *which active lenses own phase X* (dispatch, US-2). The spec leaves the lookup mechanism open (A1). Constraint set: no executable code, no build step, no generated index (constitution §3); the fix is instruction text an agent follows while reading files.
- **Decision:** **Read each fact at its single declaration site, never at a copy.**
  1. **Phase ownership → the lens's own frontmatter.** All 9 lenses already declare `phases: [...]` (verified: `lenses/*.md:4`). The three skills get the computed-set form the prior art already proposed: *"pass the path of every active lens whose own frontmatter `phases` includes `<design|qa|review>` — read each active lens's frontmatter to decide; do not work from a list of lens names given here."* The active-lens set still comes from `.spark/constitution.md` (unchanged single source of activation).
  2. **Characteristic/lens vocabulary → `lenses/README.md`'s tables.** `/charter` needs each characteristic's *detection signal*, which exists nowhere else — not in frontmatter. `agents/facilitator.md` and `templates/constitution.md` point at the *Characteristics* table as the current list instead of embedding six names; the characteristic→lens mapping likewise defers to the registry's *Available lenses* / *Two ways a lens activates* rows. The two folded-in sites take the same form against *Available lenses* (T7, T8).
  The two mechanisms are not a compromise: they are the same rule applied twice, each to the only file that actually declares the fact.
- **Scope widened to eight sites (user direction, 2026-09-17) — and why this is not a spec change:** the first draft of this plan routed the two extra sites to a disclosed open sub-case. They are now T7 and T8, fixed with the same shape as the other six. This does **not** require reopening the spec: (a) C1 already settled the framing as a *general mechanism* fix rather than a site-by-site patch, and these are the same mechanism at two more call points; (b) the spec's six-site enumeration is a snapshot of the evidence that existed at Specify time (§1's `refuted-with-finding` citations), not a fence — every AC still passes verbatim, and the success signal's falsifiable test ("none of the six sites contains a hardcoded list") remains true, now with two sites to spare; (c) AC-3.2 explicitly offers two branches, "states the gap is closed" *or* "names precisely which sub-case remains open" — widening simply takes the first branch, the one the AC prefers; (d) no §6 Out of Scope item is touched — C2 excludes the *type*-triggered vocabulary (`website`/`web-app`/`api`/`cli`/`library`) in `agents/facilitator.md`'s type list and `templates/constitution.md`'s type comment; T7/T8 edit *lens-name* lists in two different files and leave every type name alone. **T7 and T8 carry no AC of their own** — recorded here so a reviewer reads that as deliberate, not as an orphan task. They serve US-1's story statement, and they are what makes T12's unqualified claim true.
- **What still is not generic, by design:** *descriptive prose that tells a human which lenses exist* — `README.md`'s lens table (complete, 9 rows), `docs/status.md:34` and `docs/workflow.md:40-42` (both still list 8 and are stale today). These are not resolved-instruction sites: no agent reads them to decide what to dispatch, so a stale copy there cannot undispatch a lens. They are named at T11 and routed at T12, not genericized (see the alternatives table).
- **Reading of AC-1.2 (recorded so a reviewer does not score it as a miss):** the spec's success signal forbids a hardcoded lens-name list at these sites, while AC-1.2 asks the mapping to "name all characteristic-triggered lenses the registry currently lists". These are reconciled the way AC-1.1 and AC-2.x are already phrased — the *resolved* instruction names all four (`security`, `i18n`, `data`, `accessibility`) because following it yields all four. Re-embedding the four names as text would recreate the defect on its first day. T5's evidence entry must show the resolution, not assert it.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  | Alternative | Why rejected |
  |---|---|
  | Re-derive phase ownership from `lenses/README.md`'s tables (the other half of A1) | The registry **has no phases column** — it cannot answer the dispatch question at all today. Adopting it means adding one, i.e. a second copy of a fact frontmatter already owns, hand-synced on every lens edit: the exact drift this feature exists to end. Registry stays authoritative for *signals*, frontmatter for *phases*. |
  | A generated/committed index (`lenses/index.json` or a table appended to the registry) | Needs a generator to stay true (constitution §3: no tracked executable code, no build step) or hand-maintenance, which is the closed list again in JSON. Also ships a third artifact to every consumer for zero added truth. |
  | Keep the lists, add "…and check the registry for others" | The status quo plus a hedge. The literal instruction would still be a closed list (`evidence.md` T7's finding was precisely that the literal instruction is wrong), so the success signal fails by inspection. |
  | Leave `skills/spark/SKILL.md:66` and `templates/spec.md:69` as a disclosed open sub-case (this plan's first draft) | Rejected by the user, and the cost comparison agrees: two one-line edits are cheaper than a permanent caveat, and shipping a "the gap is closed, except here" claim is the same over-claim shape this feature exists to retire. Now T7/T8. |
  | Also genericize the descriptive enumerations in `README.md`'s lens table, `docs/status.md:34`, `docs/workflow.md:40-42` | Prose whose job is to tell a reader *what exists* cannot defer to a table without becoming useless — "see the registry" is not an answer to "which lenses are there". These are legitimate copies; the right mitigation is that a lens author is told to update them (T11's step 3), not that they stop naming anything. Their staleness today is a real but separate documentation defect, routed at T12. |
- **Consequences:** Adding a lens becomes a genuine add-a-file for design/qa/review dispatch, for `/charter` reachability, for `/spark`'s nudge and for the spec template's NFR guidance — no skill, agent or template edit across seven files. What remains is prose upkeep, now written down where the author reads it. Harder: dispatch is a two-hop read (constitution → each lens's frontmatter) instead of one glance, so a lens with a malformed or missing `phases` line silently drops out of every phase — the registry's "Adding a lens" step 2 must say so (T11). `demo-day`'s "browser-observable" qualifier is replaced by `phases: qa`, which is correct under constitution §8's substitute-QA-method projects but is a deliberate widening, recorded at T3.

## 4. Test Strategy

No automated suite exists and none is possible for prompt material (constitution §4). The bar is `claude plugin validate` plus a written dry run of every phase touched, negative case first — the method `accessibility-lens` used at `evidence.md` T7/T9.

- **Per task:** each DoD is closed by a **read-based re-derivation** written into `.spark/lens-dispatch-registry/evidence.md` — quote the *resolved* instruction text after the edit, then derive the lens set it yields from the 9 frontmatters, and show that set matches the expected one. Reasoning about what a file "would do" without quoting it does not close a task.
- **US-1 (T5, T6):** re-read both files against `lenses/README.md`'s Characteristics table; AC-1.4's add-a-file claim is tested by walking a hypothetical 10th lens through "Adding a lens" steps 1–3 only, and confirming no further edit is needed for `/charter` to propose its characteristic.
- **Folded-in sites (T7, T8):** same re-derivation, but the expected set is *all lenses in the registry*, not a phase subset — the test is that the resolved text yields 9 today and would yield 10 for the hypothetical lens with no edit. T8 additionally diffs `templates/spec.md` heading-by-heading against its pre-edit state (protected contract file, NFR-6).
- **US-2 (T2, T3, T4):** one chain per skill — constitution → frontmatter → resolved set → the receiving agent's head clause. T2 carries the full chain as the walking skeleton; T3/T4 reuse it and vary only the phase.
- **US-3 (T11, T12):** the disclosure is checked against the *post-fix* text of the other nine tasks, not against this plan's intent — if anything remains enumerated, it is named, not rounded off.
- **NFR-4 (T9):** the zero-omission matrix across all 9 lenses, both dispatch and receiving layers. **NFR-5/NFR-6 (T1, T10):** negative case run before and after, with `validate` output and a diff-level compatibility assertion.
- **Deliberately not verified:** the positive firing case on a real project with a characteristic-triggered lens active. No venue exists (spec A2); recorded `not-verified-live`, never asserted. Nothing here goes to `/demo-day` in a browser — this repo has no browser-observable surface (constitution §8).
